Thonglets

Classified

Playable now

Tiny creatures in thongs who think you're their god. They'll build you a statue. They'll follow you into a pit. You are not a good god.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • You are the light. Move it with the mouse, the arrow keys or a finger, and the Thonglets follow. Walk them through the brick pile to the building site and they'll build you a statue.
  • They walk round a pit, unless your light is over it. Smite (click, Space) stops rival gods with faces; a red ring means it'll land. Bless (B, Shift) hurries everyone near you.
  • A new rival god each stage, then all of them at once, then Judgement Day. Pick a commandment between stages. Today's run is the same for everyone.
