Thonglets

Classified

Playable now

Tiny creatures in thongs who think you're their god. They'll build you a statue. They'll follow you into a pit. You are not a good god.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • You are the light. Move it with the mouse, the arrow keys or a finger, and the Thonglets follow. Anywhere. That's the problem.
  • Walk them through the brick pile, then to the building site, and they'll build you a statue. Mind the pit. They won't.
  • Seven stages, each with a new rival god: the Feed, a Landlord, Space Billionaire, the Loudspeaker, the Sea and the Planning Office. Then Judgement Day.
  • Smite (click, Space) strikes the ground: it shuts rivals up and hits anyone standing there. They love you more for it. Bless (B, Shift) speeds up everyone near your light and makes them ignore the rivals for a bit.
  • Between stages, pick a commandment. Each one helps. Each one costs. Today's run is the same for everyone.
