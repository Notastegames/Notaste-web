How to play

  • You're the hand. The page scrolls itself; steer left and right with the arrow keys, A and D, the mouse or a finger. Click (Space, a mouse click or the Click button) on whatever your fingertip is touching.
  • Find the tiny Reject all on each cookie banner. Move before a newsletter pop-up lands on you, or close it with its little X. Go round adverts: nothing closes them, and clicking one opens a new tab.
  • The life story slows you right down, so keep to the white space between the words. Jump to recipe helps, sometimes. Three courses; the clock runs throughout. Today's run is the same pages for everyone.
