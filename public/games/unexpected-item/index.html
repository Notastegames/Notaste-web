Unexpected Item

Approval needed

Playable now

Scan your own shopping. The machine thinks you're stealing it. There is one assistant for every till in the shop, and she's on her way. Slowly.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • Scan each item (Space, a click, or the Scan button) as its barcode crosses the red line. Dead centre is a perfect scan. Miss it and it goes round again.
  • Bag it (B, a click on the bag, or the Bag button), but only when the scale says OK. Touch the bag while it says Wait and there's an unexpected item in the bagging area: lift the bag and put it back, or wait for Bev.
  • Loose fruit has no barcode, so pick the right picture (1 to 4, or tap it) before the machine guesses. Scissors, candles and cooking wine need Bev's approval. Today's run puts the same shopping on the belt for everyone.
