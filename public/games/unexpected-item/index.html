How to play

  • Scan on the line (Space, a click or the Scan button), then bag on OK (B, the bag or the Bag button).
  • Pay for everything before the shop shuts. Bag on Wait and the till locks. From the trolley on, misses light its suspicion: the more lights, the likelier it accuses you.
  • Accused anyway? Lift the bag and put it back, or wait for Bev. Pick loose fruit from four pictures (1 to 4, or a tap) before the machine guesses. Scissors, candles and cooking wine need Bev. Today's run is the same shopping for everyone.
