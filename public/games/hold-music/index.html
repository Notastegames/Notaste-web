Hold Music

Please hold

Playable now

Phone a company about your broadband. Remember the menu, tap along to the hold music, and get put through to someone else. Your call is important to us.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • Press your problem: when the menu reads out what's on your sticky note, press its number (1 to 9, or the keypad). On hold, tap at the ring: Space, a click or a tap anywhere, as each note reaches the ring at your ear.
  • Get through four calls. Wrong numbers, dropped calls and being cut off cost patience, and at 0 you hang up. On hold, each missed note costs a bar of signal; lose them all and you're cut off.
  • From the second call the keypad waits until every option is read, so remember your number. Between calls, pick how to get ready. Today's run is the same calls for everyone.
