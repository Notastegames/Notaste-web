Thirty Days

Go large

Playable now. Four meals and a doctor's note.

Thirty days of nothing but fast food, and every meal has to be processed. Your insides are a council office: the Liver does grease, the Pancreas does sugar, the Kidneys do salt, and nobody does lettuce.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • Food comes down the chute onto the plate. Send each item to the desk on its sticker: grease to the Liver (left arrow, A, or click or tap the desk), sugar to the Pancreas (right, D), salt to the Kidneys (down, S). Swiping from the plate works too.
  • A full in-tray spills into the pipes. So does a wrong desk, a bit, and anything left when the chute backs up. Fill the pipes and you're signed off. From dinner, Flush (Space, up, the hatch or the Flush button) sends anything down the hatch, then recharges.
  • Four meals a day. Between them, someone asks if you'd like to go large. Every answer is yes. Today's run serves the same day to everyone.
