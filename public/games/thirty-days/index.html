Thirty Days

Go large

Playable now. Four meals and a doctor's note.

Thirty days of fast food. Your insides are a council office, and the in-trays are full.Thirty days of nothing but fast food, and every meal has to be processed. Your insides are a council office: the Liver does grease, the Pancreas does sugar, the Kidneys do salt, and nobody does lettuce.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • Match the sticker: send each item to the desk wearing the same one. Grease to the Liver (left, A), sugar to the Pancreas (right, D), salt to the Kidneys (down, S). Taps, clicks and swipes work too. Lettuce goes to any desk.
  • Get through four meals to make it to bed. Wrong desks and full in-trays fill the pipes, and full pipes sign you off.
  • From dinner, Flush (Space, up or the Flush button) sends what's on the plate down the hatch, then recharges. Between meals, someone asks if you'd like to go large. Every answer is yes. Today's run is the same day for everyone.
