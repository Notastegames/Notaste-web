Leave It at the Door

Left at door

Playable now

Deliver the food. The app has promised it was already here. It has also told the customer you're outside, added a rain fee for them and a 10p weather bonus for you.

Notaste GamesFree play

How to play

  • Orders arrive by themselves. Ride to the pickup, wait while it's prepared, then take it to the door. Keys 1 to 4 ride to that order on the phone, or steer with the arrow keys or WASD (press a turn before the junction), or click or tap where to go. The bag holds three.
  • At every door, read the customer's note. The doorstep cycles through the bell, a knock, the letterbox, gate codes or the doors for the photo: press Space, click or tap when it shows what they asked for.
  • Late deliveries cost you stars, and under 4.40 the app deactivates you. Four rushes: lunch, rain, Friday night and the cup final, with a choice between each. Today's run is the same shift for everyone.
