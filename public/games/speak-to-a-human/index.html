How to play

  • Pick the reply that argues back (1 to 4, the arrows and Enter, a click or a tap). "Have you checked behind your bins?" "I don't have bins."
  • Get the distance to a human to 0% and a human refunds you. Playing along drains your battery, and at 0% your phone dies.
  • Argue back three times in a row and you can ask for a human. From the second order it offers 50p to go away: refuse and fight on. Today's run is the same chat for everyone.
