How to play

  • The help chat offers you replies. Tap the one that's true (1 to 4, or the arrows and Enter, or click). Quick true replies push the bot further from you; three in a row and you can ask for a human. Anything that plays along heals it and costs you patience.
  • It fights back. Yes or yes. Your order number, again. "I understand you're frustrated", which heals it unless you're quick. A survey. "Are you still there?" Replies that move, and replies that shrink.
  • Each order, it will offer you 50p to go away. Take it and the order ends. Refuse and fight on for the full refund. Today's run gives everyone the same chat.
