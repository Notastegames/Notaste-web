Kart racing for drivers who were promised more legroom. The cars are small. The physics has given up.

How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD. On a phone it accelerates by itself. Steer early: the car will consider it.
  • Arrow boards mean a corner. Too fast and you're on two wheels, and the tyres will say so. Faster still and you're facing the wrong way.
  • Space (or the Gas button) when the meter's full. Everyone behind you will know. Sit right behind someone and their slipstream does some of the work.
  • Three laps. First across the line wins. Getting out of the car is a separate game.
