Playable now. Four stages and a server.

Someone has replied all to the whole company. Now everyone is replying all to say stop replying all. Stop them before the server melts. Home time is 17:00.

How to play

  • Anyone with a bar over their head is typing a reply to everyone. Click or tap them before it fills. On a keyboard, every desk has a key, laid out like the office: 1 to 4 along the back, then Q to R, A to F and Z to V. The arrow keys and Enter work too.
  • Every reply that gets out fills the server and sets off two more desks. The server cools down slowly. Fill it and it melts, and the day is over.
  • Four stages, 09:00 to 17:00: your team, the department, the whole company, then the CEO's assistant. Each one starts with a notice saying what's new.
  • Mute thread (Space, or the Mute button) stops the busiest row at once, then takes a while to recharge. Between stages IT make three suggestions: each one helps, and each one costs. Today's run is the same office for everyone.
