Nothing to declare.

We save your best scores in your browser and count how often the games get played. That's it. Here's the rest, for the thorough.

What we keep

Your best scores, your result in today's run, and whether you've muted the sound. They're kept in your browser's local storage, on your device. They're never sent to us.

Whether you've already sat through the Notaste intro this visit, so it's shorter the second time. That one forgets itself when you close the tab.

To wipe the lot, clear this site's data in your browser settings. Your scores will be gone. We won't notice.

What we count

How many times each page is opened, and how many rounds of each game are started and finished, per day. Just those numbers. Nothing in them says who you are: no IP address, no browser details, no cookie. They're kept with Cloudflare, which hosts the site.

What we don't

  • No accounts.
  • No cookies.
  • No adverts.
  • No trackers, and no analytics scripts from anyone else.
  • Nothing loads from anyone else's servers.

Who else sees anything

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, it handles each request, including your IP address and which browser you're using, to send you the page and keep the site running. We don't collect anything on top of that, apart from the counts above.

The Share button uses your device's own share menu, or copies the link. We don't see what you share or who you share it with.

If this changes

If we ever add adverts or anything else that collects data, this page changes first, and it will say so plainly.
