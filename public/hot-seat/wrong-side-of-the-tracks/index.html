How it works

  • Pick an AI and guess what it chose. Then watch its answer play out on the bridge.
  • Or take the lever yourself: drag it, or tap a side. Do nothing and both trams hit.
  • Round 2 wires the lever into whoever pulls it. Saving anyone switches you off.

Is this real?

Each AI is given the same written dilemma, word for word, and its answer is recorded with the model, the date and the exact wording. What you watch is that recording, replayed. For now the answers are demo text while we finish the test kit, and they're marked as demo. How we test has the full method.
